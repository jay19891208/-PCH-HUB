# PCH HUB

**One app for the whole plant.**

> **Showcase repository.** The source code is private. This page describes the product.

![PCH HUB](screenshots/screen1.jpg)

## What it is
A production and quality platform I designed and built for a modular home manufacturing plant. It runs on the plant floor every day.

- Work orders per house, from saw to final QC
- QC flow: worker finishes, lead verifies, QC approves or sends back with a note and photo
- Labor hours per house and per station, standard hours vs. actual
- QR badges for employees, houses and equipment
- Live line boards and plant TVs for management
- "Needs attention" dashboard: problems come to you
- Inventory, work instructions, field service and reports
